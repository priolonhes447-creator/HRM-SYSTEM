About the System

This system was developed by Mr. Nhes Anthony N. Priolo on August 10, 2026.

The system was designed to simplify, improve, and streamline the applicant recruitment and hiring process of the Human Resources Department of 2GO Travel at Ever Gotesco Commonwealth Mall.

It provides a centralized platform that helps the HR team efficiently manage applicant information, recruitment stages, interview scheduling, document submissions, and hiring decisions. The system is intended to reduce manual processes, minimize errors, improve record management, and make the recruitment process faster and more organized.

The system also aims to provide applicants with a more convenient and user-friendly application experience by allowing them to submit their information and requirements digitally and monitor their recruitment status.
